Cohesive high-end playful collectible object aesthetic, polished rounded bevels, realistic plastic glass and metal, dramatic green rim lighting and soft bloom. Central compact composition, all objects fully inside canvas with generous margin, on seamless near-black petrol #031918 background with subtle emerald glow at base. No people, no NFT motifs, no logos, no words or watermarks. Square composition, large main laptop, suitable for right half of a website hero.
